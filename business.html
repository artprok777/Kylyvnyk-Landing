Is your business a good fit?

We look for businesses that can describe their service clearly, support members professionally, and offer a benefit with honest conditions.

  • Your company is active and has a public website or social presence.
  • Your offer provides a clear advantage rather than vague promotional language.
  • Availability, exclusions, booking rules, and expiry dates are disclosed.
  • Your redemption method, fulfilment process, and compatibility with other promotions are clear.
  • Your team can respond to member enquiries in a reasonable timeframe.
  • You can keep company and offer information current.

A strong member offer

Specific. Relevant. Easy to use.

Example

Complimentary 30-minute consultation for active members booking a first appointment. Subject to availability; one per member.

Avoid unclear “special conditions,” artificial discounts, or promises that cannot be consistently fulfilled.
