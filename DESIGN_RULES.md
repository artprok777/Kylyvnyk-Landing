# Kylyvnyk Club — agreed page rules

## Type and language

- Use Google Font **Prata** for the main and section headings; Geist for everything else.
- Introductory copy immediately below a section heading is 18 px. Body text and informative UI labels are at least 14 px.
- Use regular type, without italic text. Use natural title case for labels and headings, never blanket uppercase styling.
- Remove duplicate brand labels, section eyebrows, decorative microcopy, and captions that do not add information.

## Structure

- Keep the current hero headline, text, CTAs, image direction, centered logo, and language selector.
- Show three club figures in a compact row at the bottom of the hero, without a second section title or a country flag strip. Figures should match the visible directory data where applicable.
- Center section headings in gold when the layout calls for it. Use two thin gold rules fading outward on each side of a centered heading.
- Put the Top Partners link after its cards. Put the country in card details, beside the service description, rather than over the photograph.
- Use photography for the featured partners. Distinguish Business membership from Member and VIP Member through a dedicated photographic panel.
- Keep the How It Works list in its logical order; use icons rather than visible step numbers.
- Use **Lucide** icons consistently across the page. Keep the downloaded SVG files and their license in `assets/icons/`.
- Use three button tiers throughout: filled gold gradient primary, rectangular outline with the same gradient secondary, and underline-only tertiary. Do not add diagonal arrow glyphs to actions.
- Make each membership option a single clickable and keyboard-focusable card.
- Place the partner directory description below its heading. Use the search form for country selection, without quick country tabs.
- Show member offers as clear badges and keep directory card backgrounds free of noise texture.
- Present FAQs as expandable individual cards, with a visible Get in Touch action.
- Keep policy names in one compact footer row below the main columns. Until policy URLs exist, present them as labels rather than misleading links.
- Keep the Business membership card linked to `business.html`. The business page reuses the shared dark-and-gold system, FAQ component, header behaviour, and page tokens.
- Describe partner value as mechanisms—profile, offer, directory placement, introductions when available—not guaranteed customers, revenue, referrals, or rewards.
- The current partner concept is `$19.99/month`; final billing and legal terms must be confirmed before activation.
